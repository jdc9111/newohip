Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

ShiftCodes (shiftcodes.ca) is a free OHIP billing and diagnostic code reference for Ontario physicians. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with and the choices you have. We try to collect as little as possible. You can search and browse the whole site without an account.

Never enter patient information. Don't type patient names, health card numbers, dates of birth or other identifying details into ShiftCodes search boxes. Search text is processed by third-party services (see below). Describe the clinical situation in general terms only, for example "hip fracture reduction".

Information we collect

If you sign in to save favourites (optional)

  • Your email address, used to send you a one-time sign-in code and to identify your account.
  • The codes you star as favourites, with any label attached to them.
  • Basic account records kept by our sign-in provider, such as when your account was created and when you last signed in.

We don't ask for a password, your name, your billing number or any other personal details.

What you type into search

The text you type into the home page search and AI search is sent to our servers and to OpenAI to find matching codes. We don't link search text to your account. We don't intentionally keep a log of search text. Our hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs of requests.

Usage analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used: which pages are visited, roughly where visitors are located (city or region level), the type of device and browser, and how people found the site. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technology to do this. We use this information only in aggregate to improve the site.

Information stored in your browser

The site stores a few small items in your browser's local storage so it works smoothly. These include your sign-in session (if you sign in), whether you chose "Keep me signed in", a copy of your favourites for faster loading, and whether you collapsed the side menu. These items aren't used for tracking or advertising. Clearing your browser data removes them.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and sync your favourites between devices.
  • To return search results.
  • To understand which features are used and fix problems.
  • To reply when you contact us.

We don't sell your information. We don't send marketing email. Sign-in emails contain only your one-time code.

Who we share it with

We use the following service providers to run the site. Each one handles information only as needed to provide its service to us:

  • Supabase stores accounts and favourites.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails.
  • OpenAI processes search text to suggest matching codes. Under OpenAI's API terms, data sent through its API isn't used to train its models by default and may be kept for a limited time (currently up to 30 days) for abuse monitoring.
  • Google Analytics provides usage statistics.
  • Netlify hosts the website and runs our search functions.
  • Google Fonts and the jsDelivr CDN deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser. When your browser requests these files, those providers see your IP address.

If ShiftCodes shows sponsored content, it will be clearly labelled. Sponsors don't receive any personal information about our users.

We may disclose information if required by law, for example in response to a valid court order.

Where information is stored

Our service providers may store and process information outside Canada, including in the United States. Information stored there may be accessible to law enforcement and national security authorities of that country under its laws.

How long we keep it

Account information and favourites are kept until you ask us to delete your account. Google Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings. After that it is deleted automatically.

Your choices and rights

  • Access, correction or deletion. Email us to see what we hold about you, correct it, or delete your account and favourites. We'll respond within 30 days.
  • Analytics opt-out. You can block Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on or your browser's tracking protection.
  • Use without an account. Signing in is only needed for favourites.

If you're not satisfied with how we've handled a privacy concern, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Access to your favourites is restricted to your own account. No system is perfectly secure. On a shared hospital workstation, leave "Keep me signed in" unticked so you're signed out when the tab closes.

Children

ShiftCodes is intended for healthcare professionals and isn't directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we'll also note it on the site.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, email info@erfaster.ca.
