Billing value by shift

The highest-value eligible combination per encounter, by shift period. Bill what you actually did — this ranks the combinations that are billable together, it does not suggest what to claim. Fees effective April 1, 2026.

Legend

What this table encodes

  • Premiums attach to critical care and counselling (G521/523/522, G395/391, K005, K028, K623) — not to assessments and not to consultations. That is why H055 and H065 appear bare in every column.
  • Premiums do not bill with H101–H154. The time-of-day uplift is already built into the H12x night tier and the H15x weekend tier.
  • H055 and H065 are consultations — not billable with H1x2 or H1x3, but billable with an H1x4 reassessment. Hence the consult + reassessment rows.
  • Friday evening is weekend-tier. As of April 1 2026, Friday 17:00–24:00 moved out of the weekday evening group: use H15x + H113, not H13x + H114.
  • H114 is new as of April 1 2026 and did not exist on older cheat sheets. It adds $23.65 to every weekday-evening critical care and counselling row.
  • Reassessments: maximum 3 per patient per day, more than 2h apart, with a change in management.
  • Procedures are deliberately excluded. E412 (+20%) and E420 (+50%) are multipliers rather than additions, and would multiply the row count instead of adding to it.

Eligibility rules here reflect our reading of the Schedule of Benefits and are not a substitute for it. Verify against the current Schedule of Benefits before billing. Statutory holidays count as weekend-tier; this page cannot detect them, so the highlight may be wrong on a holiday.
